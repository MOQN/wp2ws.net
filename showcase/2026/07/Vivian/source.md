# Sources

Synthetic Eden uses four glTF models. Nothing else in the scene is a downloaded model. Water is the three.js Reflector. Ground, rocks, mist, fireflies, and the distant trees are written in code. The ground texture `assets/textures/turf.jpg` is a crop from the flower model's own texture.

## Downloaded from Tripo

### `assets/models/flower/flower.glb`

- Title: flower garden 3d model
- Author: Tabita
- Platform: Tripo
- Used for the flower clumps along the banks
- Description: a stylized floral garden diorama on a low oval base of moss and soil
- Link:[https://studio.tripo3d.ai/3d-model/colorful-flower-garden-with-lilies-daisies-and-other-blooms-on-a-mos-0a0c9353-b028-4016-8f81-33724c832809](https://studio.tripo3d.ai/3d-model/colorful-flower-garden-with-lilies-daisies-and-other-blooms-on-a-mos-0a0c9353-b028-4016-8f81-33724c832809)



### `assets/models/bush/bush.glb`

- Title: bush 3d model
- Author: ochenkai
- Platform: Tripo
- Used for the bushes along the banks
- Description: a stylized shrub, a dense rounded mound of branching stems
